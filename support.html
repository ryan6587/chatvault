Support

Export says "No messages found"

The chat site may have changed its layout. Reload the page and try again. If it still fails, email us. We check for layout changes every day.

How do I get my full archive?

  • ChatGPT: Settings → Data controls → Export data
  • Claude: Settings → Privacy → Export data
  • Gemini: takeout.google.com → My Activity → Gemini Apps (JSON)

Then open ChatVault → Import archive and choose the downloaded file.

Activating Pro

After buying ChatVault Pro ($3/month) or ChatVault Pro (Yearly) ($24/year), copy the key that starts with CHATVAULT from your receipt or the Polar customer portal. Then open the ChatVault popup, paste it and click Activate.

"Already active on 3 devices"

Each key works on 3 devices. On a device you no longer use, open the popup and click Deactivate this device. You can also remove the activation in the customer portal.
