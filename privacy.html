Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Summary: your chats never leave your device. ChatVault Exporter does not collect, transmit, sell or share your conversations, and it has no analytics or tracking.

What the extension reads

  • Single-chat export: the conversation shown in the tab you are viewing on chatgpt.com, claude.ai or gemini.google.com, and only when you click an export button. The extension reads what is already on the page. It makes no requests to those sites.
  • Archive import (Pro): the official data-export file (.zip or .json) that you choose from your own computer. It is read inside your browser and never uploaded.

Where your data goes

Every export is created inside your browser and saved to your device through Chrome's download feature. We run no server that receives chat content.

License checks (Pro only)

ChatVault Pro is sold through Polar (polar.sh), which acts as merchant of record, as ChatVault Pro ($3/month) or ChatVault Pro (Yearly) ($24/year). Each license key works on up to 3 devices. When you activate a key, the extension sends Polar only: the key, our organization ID, a generic device label ("ChatVault browser extension") and, on later checks, the activation ID Polar returned. It re-checks about once a day and keeps working offline for up to 7 days. Deactivating frees the device slot. No chat content, browsing history or other data is sent. Polar receives the email and payment details you give at checkout, under Polar's privacy policy.

Storage

The extension stores only your settings, license key and activation ID in Chrome's local extension storage on your device. Uninstalling the extension deletes them.

Permissions

  • storage: keep settings and license status.
  • activeTab: read the chat tab you are exporting from when you click the toolbar button.
  • downloads: save export files to your device.
  • Site access to chatgpt.com, claude.ai and gemini.google.com only: read the chat you choose to export.

Limited Use

The use of information received by ChatVault Exporter complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the export feature you request.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
